References
==========

.. [Reddy2024] J. N. Reddy, *Computational Methods in Engineering: Finite
   Difference, Finite Volume, Finite Element, and Dual Mesh Control Domain
   Methods*, CRC Press, Boca Raton, 2024.  The method implemented here, and
   every verification case in :doc:`verification`, comes from this book.

.. [Reddy2019] J. N. Reddy, *Introduction to the Finite Element Method*, 4th
   edition, McGraw-Hill, New York, 2019.

.. [ReddyTheories] J. N. Reddy, *Theories and Analyses of Beams and
   Axisymmetric Circular Plates*, CRC Press, Boca Raton, 2022.  Source of the
   beam and circular plate theories used in the structural module.

.. [ReddyPlates] J. N. Reddy, *Theory and Analysis of Elastic Plates and
   Shells*, 2nd edition, CRC Press, Boca Raton, 2007.

.. [ReddyFluids] J. N. Reddy and D. K. Gartling, *The Finite Element Method in
   Heat Transfer and Fluid Dynamics*, 3rd edition, CRC Press, Boca Raton,
   2010.  Source of the penalty formulation used in the fluids module.

.. [Ghia1982] U. Ghia, K. N. Ghia and C. T. Shin, "High-Re solutions for
   incompressible flow using the Navier-Stokes equations and a multigrid
   method", *Journal of Computational Physics*, 48(3):387-411, 1982.  Reference
   data for the lid-driven cavity.

.. [MOOSE] C. J. Permann et al., "MOOSE: Enabling massively parallel multiphysics
   simulation", *SoftwareX*, 11:100430, 2020.  The framework whose object model
   inspired the structure of this library.

.. [Eigen] G. Guennebaud, B. Jacob et al., *Eigen v3*, 2010,
   https://eigen.tuxfamily.org.

.. [pybind11] W. Jakob, J. Rhinelander and D. Moldovan, *pybind11 — Seamless
   operability between C++11 and Python*, 2017,
   https://github.com/pybind/pybind11.

.. [meshio] N. Schlömer et al., *meshio: Tools for mesh files*,
   https://github.com/nschloe/meshio.
